# Privacy Policy — Modern Media Player

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

Modern Media Player ("the app") plays video and audio files stored on your device. This policy
explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

## Summary

**The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.** It has no
internet permission, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything stays on your device.

## What the app accesses on your device

| Access | Why | Leaves your device? |
|---|---|---|
| Videos and audio (media permission) | To list and play your media files | No |
| Files you choose to rename, copy, move, delete or hide | Only when you ask, after Android's own confirmation | No |
| Fingerprint / face / screen lock (optional App lock) | To unlock the app. Handled entirely by Android; the app never sees biometric data | No |
| Notifications | To show playback controls while audio plays | No |

## Data the app keeps on your device

- Playback history (resume positions, recently played), favorites and playlists.
- Settings, and — if you turn on App lock with an app PIN — a salted one-way hash of the PIN
  (never the PIN itself).
- Hidden folders: files you choose to hide are moved into the app's private storage.

You can clear the watch history in Settings → Privacy & security. Uninstalling the app deletes all
of this data, **including hidden files** (unhide them first to keep them).

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
